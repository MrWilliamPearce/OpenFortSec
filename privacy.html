Privacy notice

What personal data this website handles, why, and the rights you have under UK data protection law.

Who we are

OpenFortSec is an independent cybersecurity startup founded by William. It is not yet a registered company. William is responsible for how personal data is handled on this website, which makes him the “controller” under UK data protection law.

To contact us about your data, send us a message through one of the official channels on our Find Us page.

In short

This website has no forms, accounts or newsletters, and we don’t ask you for any personal information. We don’t sell personal data or use it for advertising.

DataWhyLawful basis

Who we share data with

  • GitHub hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Google provides Google Analytics, but only receives data if analytics is in use and you accept analytics cookies.
  • X and Instagram handle any messages you send us on those platforms, under their own privacy policies.

We don’t share personal data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

International transfers

GitHub, Google, X and Instagram may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK–US data bridge or the UK’s international data transfer agreement and addendum.

How long we keep data

  • Hosting logs are kept by GitHub in line with its own retention policy.
  • Google Analytics data, if collected, is kept for no more than 14 months. Its cookies expire after up to 2 years, or straight away if you withdraw consent.
  • Messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data deleted
  • restrict or object to how we use your data
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting anything done before

To use any of these rights, contact us through the Find Us page. It’s free, and we’ll respond within one month.

We don’t make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

How we protect data

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS, uses a strict content security policy, and loads no third-party scripts unless you accept analytics cookies.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator: visit ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or call 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

We’ll update this notice when how we handle data changes, and show the date of the latest version below.
