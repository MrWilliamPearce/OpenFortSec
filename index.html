Our approach
How an engagement works
Every engagement follows the same disciplined sequence, so you always know what is being tested, why, and what happens next.


    

  1. 1
    Scope
    We agree what is in scope, the rules of engagement and what success looks like for you.
    2. 

  2. 2
    Assess
    We test and review methodically, within the agreed limits, recording evidence as we go.
    3. 

  3. 3
    Report
    Findings are ranked by real risk, with evidence and clear remediation steps.
    4. 

  4. 4
    Improve
    We walk your team through the results and can retest once fixes are in place.

What you receive
Findings you can act on
Every engagement ends with a report written for two audiences: a clear summary for decision-makers and full technical detail for the people fixing things.


  • An executive summary in plain language
  • Evidence for every finding, ranked by real risk
  • Step-by-step remediation guidance
  • A walkthrough with your team and an optional retest
