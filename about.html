About OpenFortSec

A growing cybersecurity startup: ambitious about what it can build, and honest about where it is today.

Our purpose

Why OpenFortSec exists

Too much security advice is either too vague to act on or too technical to understand. OpenFortSec was founded to close that gap.

We help organisations see where they are exposed, decide what matters most and make improvements that last. We are a young company, and we intend to earn trust through the quality of our work rather than claims about it.

Approach

Responsible practice

Our commitments

Security testing involves trust. These commitments govern every engagement.

We test only with written authorisation and within the agreed scope.

We protect client data and findings, and share them only with the people you name.

We disclose vulnerabilities responsibly and never use findings for anything else.

We are honest about what we can deliver, and recommend specialists when needed.
